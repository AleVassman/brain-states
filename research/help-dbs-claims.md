## What is reconstruction in this episode

All findings come from one study of 8 people who already had the electrodes, scanned with the stimulator off and on, so "before" here means switched off, not before surgery. The premotor cortex, also more active, is not a separate area in the brain model. The supplementary motor area finding is on the same side as the stimulation, during movement of the hand on the other side. The subthalamic nucleus itself is not drawn. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.

## Notes from the research pass

- "Before treatment" is the stimulator switched off in the same people, not before surgery; the phase names say "Stimulator off".
- The supplementary motor area increase was ipsilateral to stimulation; the region is drawn on both sides because patients were stimulated on different sides and the abstract gives no side breakdown.
- Read as abstract only: Ceballos-Baumann et al. 1999.
