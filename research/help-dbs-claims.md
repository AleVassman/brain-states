## What is reconstruction in this episode

All findings come from one study of 8 people who already had the electrodes, scanned with the stimulator off and on, so this is a comparison of on and off, not of before and after surgery. The premotor cortex, also more active, is not a separate area in the brain model. The supplementary motor area finding is on the same side as the stimulation; the abstract does not say which side or sides were stimulated, so drawing both sides is an assumption. The subthalamic nucleus itself is not drawn. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.
