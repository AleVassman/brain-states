## What is reconstruction in this episode

This is one randomised trial of 216 people, with 174 analysed as planned, at 28 sites in France. The hippocampus still shrank with donepezil, only more slowly. Memory and thinking tests did not differ between the groups after a year, so slower shrinkage did not show up as better memory within the trial. A second report from the same trial found slower shrinkage of the basal forebrain, which is not a separate area in the brain model. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.

## The condition

### **hippocampus** — Mixed evidence

Claim: Shrank over a year in people with suspected very early Alzheimer's disease: by about 3.5% with placebo and about 1.9% with donepezil, in a randomised trial of 174 people analysed as planned. Memory and thinking tests did not differ. Brightness stands for volume lost over the year.
