## What is reconstruction in this episode

This is one randomised trial: 216 people at 28 sites in France, with results reported for the 174 who kept to the trial protocol. The participants did not have dementia, and their diagnosis was suspected, not confirmed; the abstract does not say how it was established. The Alzheimer's episode shows activity, while this one shows tissue loss. The hippocampus still shrank with donepezil, only more slowly, and memory and thinking tests did not differ between the groups after a year. A second report by the same study group, apparently from the same trial, found slower shrinkage of the basal forebrain, which is not a separate area in the brain model. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.

## The condition

### **hippocampus** — Mixed evidence

Claim: Shrank in people with suspected very early Alzheimer's disease: by about 3.5% a year with placebo and about 1.9% a year with donepezil, in a randomised trial (174 people who kept to the protocol). Memory and thinking tests did not differ. Brightness stands for the yearly loss of volume.
