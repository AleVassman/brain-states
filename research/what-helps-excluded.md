# What helps: treatments looked at and not included

The rule (from `docs/2026-11-18-what-helps-design.md` §5): a treatment gets an episode only if at least one study, or a pooled analysis, scanned humans before and after that treatment and reports a regional change with a direction, on a region the brain model has.

| Treatment | Condition | Why it is not included |
|---|---|---|
| Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) | Depression | The before-and-after study found (Taylor et al. 2018, sham-controlled, doi:10.1016/j.jad.2018.02.019) reports "no specific effect of rTMS (sham vs active) on connectivity"; changes in the subgenual cingulate went with improvement in both the real and the sham group. The most-cited TMS imaging papers (Fox et al. 2012, 2013) predict who will respond rather than measuring change after treatment. Drawing a TMS-specific change would not be supported. |
| CBT for PTSD (Felmingham et al. 2007) | PTSD | The study is relevant, but no abstract or full text could be retrieved to read. PTSD is covered by the exposure therapy episode. |
