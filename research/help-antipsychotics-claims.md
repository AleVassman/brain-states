## What is reconstruction in this episode

All findings come from one study of 24 people in a first episode of psychosis, treated with risperidone or aripiprazole, and 24 people without psychosis scanned twice. The changes are those that went with improvement in psychosis, at rest; they show how closely areas work together, not activity. The striatum is drawn as the caudate because the source names "striatal regions" without saying which; that is an assumption. The source names the anterior cingulate and the parietal lobe without saying which parts; they are drawn on the dorsal anterior cingulate and the inferior parietal cortex. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.
