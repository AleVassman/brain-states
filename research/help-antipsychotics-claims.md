## What is reconstruction in this episode

All findings come from one study of 24 people in a first episode of psychosis, treated with risperidone or aripiprazole, and 24 people without psychosis scanned twice. Only one link changed across the whole group; the other findings link the size of the change in each person to how much their psychosis improved. Before treatment, the striatum's links did not differ significantly from people without psychosis. The right ventral striatum is drawn as the right caudate, an approximation. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.
