## What is reconstruction in this episode

This is one small study: five people in a court-supervised rehabilitation programme who stayed off the drug for over a year, scanned twice with PET. They were the five of twelve who stayed drug-free, and those who dropped out had lower transporter levels early on, so the five may not be typical. The loss in the first part compares 12 people early in abstinence with 11 controls. The authors consider that the early loss may partly be a temporary adjustment rather than damage. Recovery of the transporters did not bring equal recovery in memory and movement tests. It is about methamphetamine only, and about staying off it, not a specific treatment. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.

## Notes from the research pass

- Not a specific therapy: the "treatment" is protracted abstinence within a supervised programme; the caveat says so.

- Linked to "Addiction and craving"; the craving episode is not specific to methamphetamine, and the caveat says this one is.
