## What is reconstruction in this episode

All findings come from one study of 15 people with temporal lobe epilepsy, 10 of whom became seizure-free, and 15 people without epilepsy; the authors call it a preliminary analysis. In the 5 who still had seizures no change was detected, but that group was very small. The weaker connections before surgery rest mainly on this group's earlier work. A third arousal centre, the ventral tegmental area, did not change. The arousal centres that changed are two small nuclei in the upper brainstem, drawn as the brainstem outline. The cortex areas are mapped approximately: the inferior frontal gyrus to the ventrolateral prefrontal cortex, the precentral gyrus to the motor cortex, the postcentral gyrus to the sensory cortex. The superior parietal lobule is not in the brain model. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.
