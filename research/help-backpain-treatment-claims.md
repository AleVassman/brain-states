## What is reconstruction in this episode

This is one study: 18 patients and 16 people without pain were compared before treatment, and 14 patients were scanned again six months later. The thickening was significant only in the 11 who improved on pain or disability by any amount; across all 14 there was no significant change. Treatment was spine surgery or facet joint injections, so this shows pain relief in general, not one treatment, and the authors note a placebo effect may have contributed. Follow-up was six months. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.
