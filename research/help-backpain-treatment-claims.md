## What is reconstruction in this episode

This is one study of 14 people treated with spine surgery or facet joint injections, so it shows what happens when pain is relieved, not the effect of one particular treatment. More thickening went with more pain relief, which is a relationship across people. Other areas, the primary motor cortex and the right anterior insula, thickened more in people whose disability or pain improved more; those are described, not drawn as group changes. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.
