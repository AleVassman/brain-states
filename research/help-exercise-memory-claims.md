## What is reconstruction in this episode

These are studies of mild cognitive impairment, not of Alzheimer's disease itself, and they do not show that exercise prevents or slows Alzheimer's. The volume finding comes from a trial of 86 women aged 70 to 80, of whom only 29 had brain scans both before and after, compared with balance and toning exercises; in that trial a larger increase in the left hippocampus went, unexpectedly, with worse performance on one memory measure. The blood-flow finding comes from a study of 17 people with mild cognitive impairment and 18 without. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.
