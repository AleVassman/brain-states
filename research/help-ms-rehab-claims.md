## What is reconstruction in this episode

All findings come from one trial of 23 people with multiple sclerosis and attention problems: 12 did the training and 11 a placebo programme. The superior parietal lobule is not a separate area in the brain model and is not drawn. The training does not change the multiple sclerosis itself. The first part is drawn at rest because the comparison starts from each person's own level. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.
