## What is reconstruction in this episode

All findings come from one study of 26 people with migraine, scanned between attacks during a test with a painful stimulus to the face, not during a migraine attack. A related antibody that blocks the CGRP receptor instead (erenumab) produced a different pattern in an earlier study, so the brain effect depends on the antibody. The source reports no difference from people without migraine, so the first part is drawn at rest. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.
