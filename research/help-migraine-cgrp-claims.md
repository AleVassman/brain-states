## What is reconstruction in this episode

All findings come from one study of 26 people with migraine, without a comparison group of people without migraine. Some had a headache on one or both scan days. The larger reduction in people whose migraines improved, meaning 30% fewer headache days after three months, comes from a subgroup of 15 who had the same headache state at both scans. A related antibody that blocks the CGRP receptor instead (erenumab) acted on a different network in an earlier study, so the brain effect depends on the antibody. These are averages across groups of people, not a picture of any one person's brain. Treatments help many people, not everyone, and in different ways. This is not medical advice: talk to a doctor or therapist about what is right for you.

## Notes from the research pass
